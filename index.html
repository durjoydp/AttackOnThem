COMMANDER ABILITIES

NEON BASTION

Defend the core. Choose your towers wisely, combine elements, and survive every wave.

How to play: Pick a tower (or press 1-7) and click an empty tile to build. Enemies follow the glowing path - if they reach your core you lose lives.
Combos: Cryo-slowed enemies take +25% damage from Cannon & Railgun (shatter). Burning enemies can't be healed. Tesla ignores armor. Beacons boost every tower around them.
Flyers ignore the path - Cannon and Inferno cannot hit them. Send waves early (Space) for bonus gold. Use abilities (Q / W / E) in emergencies. Right-click or Esc cancels placement.
